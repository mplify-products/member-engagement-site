Track · Structure
Members organize in five committees
All members can participate in all committee work; each committee runs the projects that create Mplify Assets.
Commercial & Business
Business requirements and use-case definitions — the main input to the Digital Services and LSO committees.
Digital Services
NaaS-related services, attributes, and the supporting standards for the Mplify Assets library.
LSO
NaaS automation in the Lifecycle Service Orchestration (LSO) framework — APIs and Agentic LSO.
Marketing New
Meta-assets that explain and present Mplify's work to members and the wider NaaS industry.
Test & Certification
The test and certification requirements that Mplify Assets must meet to become certified.

Drive · Leadership
Three leadership routes
Members drive Mplify's work at three layers — steering a whole committee, leading a single project, and representing Mplify in their region.
01
Committee
leadership
Who leads
One or more Co-Chairs — typically two — appointed by the Board of Directors, usually each June.
What they do
Own committee agendas, decisions, and compliance — and appoint and oversee the Project Leads within the committee.
02
Project
leadership
Who leads
One or more Project Leads, appointed by and reporting to the committee Co-Chairs.
What they do
Build the participant group, drive the deliverable through the monthly sprints, and initiate the ballots.
03
Regional
leadership
Who leads
Regional chapters, ambassadors, or regional representatives.
What they do
Drive engagement and represent Mplify in their part of the world.
