Mplify Alliance

Principle

Members drive the work of Mplify

The alliance’s standards, APIs, certifications, and supporting assets are created by members, in member-led committees and projects. Staff enable the process; members steer the substance.

Mplify Alliance

Cadence

A monthly rhythm, on purpose

One-month sprints

Short cycles keep scope visible, make progress inspectable, and let members join or rejoin without waiting for a yearly cycle.

End-of-month voting

Regular ballots convert project output into alliance decisions — so work does not stall in draft form.

The goal is speed with accountability: members move work forward together, then formally approve it.

Mplify Alliance

Focus

Three major areas of work

NaaS for AI

Defining the network services and attributes required for AI-ready infrastructure.

NaaS Federation

Enabling multi-provider NaaS that can be offered, ordered, and operated across organizational boundaries.

Extending Lifecycle Service Orchestration so automated and agentic systems can act on LSO APIs.

Tooling
